GROUP BY & HAVING : 

GROUP BY is used to group rows having the same values in one or more columns.
It is mainly used with aggregate functions like:
COUNT()
SUM()
AVG()
MAX()
MIN()

Query : select CourseName, Count(CourseName) as aaa from tkastd group by CourseName having avg(totalFees) >45000;

ref Queries of Group by & having : 


SELECT productName,SUM(Quantity) AS totalC FROM shop GROUP BY ProductName HAVING SUM(Quantity) >= 5 ;

SELECT productName,SUM(Quantity) AS totalC FROM shop GROUP BY ProductName HAVING totalc > 5 AND  totalC < 7;

 SELECT productName,SUM(Quantity) AS totalC FROM shop WHERE price >= 30000  GROUP BY ProductName;

 SELECT productName,SUM(Quantity) AS totalC FROM shop GROUP BY ProductName ORDER BY totalC ASC;

Count employees in each department --> 

SELECT department, COUNT(*) AS total_emp
FROM employee
GROUP BY department;



Total salary per department --> 

SELECT department, SUM(salary) AS total_salary
FROM employee
GROUP BY department;



Employees count by department and city --> 

SELECT department, city, COUNT(*) AS total
FROM employee
GROUP BY department, city;



Departments with more than 2 employees --> 

SELECT department, COUNT(*) AS total_emp
FROM employee
GROUP BY department
HAVING COUNT(*) > 2;




Departments with salary > 100000  -->

SELECT department, SUM(salary) AS total_salary
FROM employee
GROUP BY department
HAVING SUM(salary) > 100000;



Pune employees grouped by department with salary > 50000 --> 

SELECT department, SUM(salary) AS total_salary
FROM employee
WHERE city = 'Pune'
GROUP BY department
HAVING SUM(salary) > 50000;
